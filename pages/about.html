Our parish

Rooted in faith, welcoming in spirit.

At Mater Dei Cathedral Parish, we come together to worship God, celebrate the sacraments, and accompany one another on our journey of faith. Whether you are a longtime parishioner or visiting for the first time, we are glad you are here.

Our parish life is shaped by prayer, community, and a shared call to serve with compassion.
